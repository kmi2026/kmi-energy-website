Our Core Business

Five major business segments building a full life-cycle guarantee system for energy and industrial equipment

01

Soundproofing Solutions

Targeting the noise control needs of energy and industrial equipment. Providing standardized and customized soundproof enclosures for gas turbine OEMs like GE and Siemens Energy. Offering turnkey solutions from acoustic design, product R&D to CFD thermal/ventilation analysis.

02

Distributed Energy Solutions

DataPower's flexible modular power generation systems can be deployed in weeks. An on-site core energy engine specifically designed to support AI computing centers, large industrial, and mining projects.

03

Gas Regulating Systems

As a key front-end device for gas turbine power systems, it is responsible for stabilizing, regulating, and filtering high-pressure natural gas. It's the 'brain and safety valve' of the combustion system, ensuring impurity-free natural gas injection and preventing pressure fluctuation damage.
